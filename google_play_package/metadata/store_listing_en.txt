Welcome to StarWireHub 🚀 — Your smart gateway to discovering trending open-source projects, top GitHub repositories, and reliable free software alternatives (FOSS).

Whether you are a software engineer seeking cutting-edge AI libraries, developer tools, or a privacy enthusiast looking to replace proprietary commercial software with self-hosted alternatives, StarWireHub delivers a seamless, responsive, and richly curated experience.

✨ Key Features
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔥 Trending Velocity & Smart Discovery:
• Track repositories experiencing rapid weekly growth and surges in star counts.
• Flexible sorting: Weekly Growth Velocity, Most Stars, Most Forks, and Recently Added.
• Filter by category (AI Agents, Databases, Mobile & Desktop Apps, Security, Dev Tools) and programming languages (Rust, Go, TypeScript, Python, Dart, etc.).

⚡ Open-Source Alternatives (FOSS Library):
• Curated directory of open-source alternatives to commercial software.
• Find self-hostable replacements for tools like Notion, Figma, Slack, 1Password, and CleanMyMac.
• Take control of your data and workflow with verified community projects.

🤖 Intelligent Arabic & English Summaries:
• Instant conceptual summaries explaining what the project does, why it is surging, and its primary use cases.
• One-tap bilingual toggle between Arabic explanation and original English metadata.

📊 Real-Time GitHub Integration:
• Live repository metrics: Real-time stars, forks, open issues, watchers count, primary language, and open-source license.
• Built-in full Markdown README.md viewer with formatted code blocks, images, and clickable links.
• One-click terminal clone commands (git clone) and quickstart instructions.

🔔 Hourly Background Sync & Rich Notifications:
• Background checks every 60 minutes to alert you when exciting new repositories or releases are indexed.
• Expanded, rich notifications (BigTextStyle) providing full project details, growth rate, and category right in your status bar.

📱 Responsive Tablet & Mobile Design:
• Clean, modern Light Theme engineered for optimal visual comfort.
• Adaptive layout: Persistent Arabic/English sidebar navigation and multi-column grid dashboard on tablets (7" & 10"+), fluid bottom navigation on phones.
• Customizable Arabic typography: Switch between Cairo, Tajawal, Almarai, and Alexandria directly in Settings.

💾 Bookmarks & Offline Cache:
• Save favorite repositories to your personal bookmarks with local persistence.
• Browse previously loaded projects even without an active internet connection.
